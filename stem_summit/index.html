One-hour plan: an Hour of AI, speech edition

Fits Code.org’s Hour of AI in early December, or any science or computer science class.

  1. See Your Voice (10 min). Whistle, hiss and whisper. Big idea: a computer only gets a wiggle of air pressure.
  2. Spectrogram Detective (15 min). Read sound pictures with clues. Big idea: sounds leave patterns a machine can learn.
  3. Be the Recognizer (15 min). Be the voice assistant in quiet and noise. Big idea: noise and rare words cause errors, measured as word error rate.
  4. Human vs. machine (10 min). Read the same speaker cards to Windows Live Captions or phone voice typing, paste what it wrote into the online scorer, and compare error rates. Follow your school’s technology policy.
  5. Careers (5 min). Each of the seven systems inside a voice assistant is a STEM career (slide 7). Who will fix what voice assistants still get wrong?
